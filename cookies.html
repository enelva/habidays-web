Cookie policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

Habidays keeps its website simple and does not use cookies for analytics, advertising or tracking.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information that a website can store on your device. They can be used for things such as remembering preferences, keeping users signed in, measuring website usage or supporting security features.

Cookies on habidays.com

Habidays does not currently use:

  • analytics cookies
  • advertising or marketing cookies
  • behavioural tracking
  • personalised advertising, or
  • cookies to build profiles of website visitors

We do not use Google Analytics or similar analytics services.

The website is hosted and delivered through Cloudflare. Cloudflare may use strictly necessary technical measures, including cookies in some circumstances, to deliver the website securely and protect it against malicious traffic.

These technical cookies are not used by Habidays for advertising or to track your activity for marketing purposes.

Because Habidays does not currently use optional cookies, there is no cookie-consent banner on the website.

Changes to this policy

If we introduce analytics, advertising or another feature that uses optional cookies in the future, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required before those cookies are used.
