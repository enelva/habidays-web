A pocket-sized bullet journal for your habits.

Everything you track, on one page you can scroll through. No dashboards to decode, no streaks to break. Just your days, in colour, wherever you are.

Your habits, your days, all on one page.

Habidays takes inspiration from classic bullet journal habit trackers, where days are rows, habits are columns, and each square shows how that habit went on that day. Over time, a picture starts to appear.

Instead of jumping between charts and separate habit screens, you can simply scroll back and see how things have been going over days, weeks and months.

Each square can be red, orange, yellow, green or somewhere in between. Because life has nuances, and your habits do too.

Add notes along the way and make Habidays truly yours.

Habits are more than just done or not done.

With Habidays, you can track them in any way, as a yes/no, number, scale, percentage, time or mood.

Reading for 5 minutes? Drinking enough water? Getting enough sleep? How are you feeling today?

Different habits need different answers. 🌱
