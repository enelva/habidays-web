for your habits.

Your habits are more than done or not done.

Habidays helps you track how things actually went. Set your own goal, choose how you want to measure it, and track anything from yes/no to numbers, percentages, time, scales or mood.

If your goal is 10 000 steps and you reach 8 000, that’s not a failed day. It’s real progress.

All your habits on one page

Days are rows, habits are columns, and each entry shows how that habit went.

Scroll through days, weeks and months without opening separate habit screens. Add notes along the way and keep your whole journal together in one place.

Track each habit exactly how you want

Not every habit is simply done or not done.

Use yes/no, numbers, scales, percentages, time or mood depending on what you want to track. Sleep, water, reading, exercise or simply how you felt that day can all be tracked differently.

See your habits develop over time

Inspired by real bullet journals, Habidays brings your days, habits and patterns together in one place so you can look back and see how things have been going.

Some days are great. Some are somewhere in between. They all count.
