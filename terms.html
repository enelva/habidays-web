1. About Habidays

Habidays is a habit-tracking and journaling app created and operated by Lars Mikael Brunnberg, based in Sweden.

Habidays helps you create habits, record entries and notes, and look back at your habits and patterns over time.

Habidays is available in two levels:

Free provides local-only use without a Habidays account.

Pro is a paid subscription that includes account creation, cloud backup and synchronisation, multiple-device use, unlimited active habits and additional Pro features.

2. Habidays Free

You can use Habidays without creating an account or purchasing a subscription.

Free is intended to let you use Habidays locally on your device with a limited number of active habits.

Free includes the core habit-tracking experience, including supported tracking types, local journal history and other features shown as included in the app.

Your Free journal is stored locally on your device and is not backed up to a Habidays cloud account.

Because we do not have a cloud copy of a Free journal, we cannot restore it if the local data is lost.

This can happen, for example, if you delete the app, clear its data, lose or replace your device, or the device becomes damaged.

The number of active habits or features available in Free may change over time. The current limits will be shown in the app.
