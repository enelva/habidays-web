Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

Habidays is designed to help you track your habits while keeping your personal information as private as reasonably possible.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Habidays processes, what stays on your device, what is stored online if you subscribe to Habidays Pro and create an account, why we process that information, how long we keep it, and what rights and choices you have.

This policy applies to the Habidays mobile app, the Habidays website at habidays.com, and related support services.

1. Who we are

Habidays is created and operated by Lars Mikael Brunnberg, based in Sweden.

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Lars Mikael Brunnberg is the data controller for personal data processed by Habidays.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your personal data is handled, you can contact hello.habidays@gmail.com.

2. Free and Pro use Habidays differently

Habidays is built as a local-first app. Your journal is stored on your device first.

Free

The Free version of Habidays is used without creating a Habidays account.

Your habits, entries, notes, settings and journal history are stored locally on your device. They are not synced to a Habidays cloud account.

Free users can use Habidays with a limited number of active habits.

Because there is no Habidays account, Free users do not provide an email address for Habidays authentication and do not receive account-related emails from us.

Pro

Habidays Pro includes account creation, cloud backup and synchronisation, multiple-device use, unlimited active habits and additional Pro features.

If you upgrade from Free to Pro and create an account, you can choose to move your existing local journal into your Habidays account. From that point, journal information associated with your account is also stored in our cloud service so that Habidays can provide backup and synchronisation.

3. Information we process

Journal information

Habidays lets you create your own habits and record information about them.

Depending on how you use the app, your journal may contain:

  • habit names;
  • tracking values and answers;
  • dates;
  • notes;
  • goals and settings;
  • colours and visual preferences; and
  • other information you choose to enter.

For Free users, this information remains in the app's local storage on your device and is not sent to Habidays for cloud storage.

For Pro users with an account, journal information is also stored and synchronised through our cloud service.

We also process technical information required for synchronisation, such as item identifiers, update timestamps and deletion markers.

Account information

If you subscribe to Habidays Pro and create an account, we process information necessary to create and maintain that account, including:

  • your email address;
  • an account identifier;
  • authentication information;
  • account and subscription status; and
  • information required to keep your account secure.

Authentication is provided through Supabase.

Habidays does not store your password in readable form.

Your email address is also used for necessary account messages such as verification codes, password resets, email-change confirmations and security-related messages.

Subscription information

Habidays Pro is purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play.

We use RevenueCat to manage subscription entitlements and determine whether your Habidays account has access to Pro.

Depending on the platform and transaction, RevenueCat may process information such as:

  • an app user or customer identifier;
  • subscription status;
  • the subscription product purchased;
  • transaction and purchase information;
  • renewal and expiry information; and
  • purchase history necessary to manage your entitlement.

Habidays does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

Payment processing is handled by Apple or Google using the payment method associated with your relevant store account.

Support information

If you contact Habidays support, we receive the email address you contact us from and any information you choose to include in your message or attachments.

Support email is handled through Gmail.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information in a support request unless it is necessary for us to help you.

Technical and security information

When Habidays communicates with online services, limited technical information may be processed automatically.

This can include information such as:

  • IP address;
  • device or operating-system information;
  • app version;
  • request timestamps; and
  • technical information necessary to operate, troubleshoot, protect and secure the service.

When you visit habidays.com, our hosting and network provider may also process basic request and connection information necessary to deliver and protect the website.

Security and operation

Support

We process information you send to us in order to answer your questions and provide support.

Legal obligations

We may process or retain information where required to comply with applicable legal, accounting, tax, fraud-prevention or regulatory obligations.

5. Sensitive information you choose to enter

Habidays does not require you to provide health information or other sensitive personal data.

However, because you choose what habits and notes to create, you may choose to enter information that is considered sensitive under data-protection law.

For example, information you enter could reveal something about your physical or mental health, religious beliefs or other areas that receive additional protection under the GDPR.

If you use Habidays Free, this journal information remains locally on your device and is not stored in a Habidays cloud account.

If you use Habidays Pro and choose to sync journal information that contains special-category personal data, we process that information only to provide the cloud storage, backup and synchronisation features you have requested.

Where required under the GDPR, this processing is based on your explicit consent.

Consent to processing sensitive journal information is separate from accepting the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

You can withdraw this consent at any time.

Because cloud synchronisation cannot continue if we no longer have permission to process synced journal content that may contain sensitive information, withdrawing this consent means stopping cloud storage and synchronisation. You can return to local-only Habidays use and have your Habidays account and associated cloud journal data deleted.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before the consent was withdrawn.

Habidays does not use your journal content for advertising or to make automated decisions about you that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

8. How long we keep information

Free users

Journal information belonging to Free users is stored locally on their device.

Habidays does not hold a cloud copy of this journal.

Local information remains on the device until it is removed through the app, the app's data is cleared, or the app is removed, subject to how the device and operating system manage app storage and backups.

Pro accounts

We normally retain account information and synced journal information for as long as your Habidays account remains active.

When you delete a synced journal item, Habidays may retain a deletion marker for up to 30 days so that the deletion can be correctly synchronised between devices. The deletion marker is then purged.

Account deletion

If you delete your Habidays account, the account and associated Habidays cloud journal information are deleted from our active systems.

Data stored locally on your own devices may need to be removed separately.

We also take reasonable steps to remove or disconnect account-related data held by service providers where appropriate.

Some limited information may still need to be retained where required by law, for example records relating to transactions, accounting, fraud prevention or legal obligations.

Some information may also remain temporarily in backups or technical systems until those copies expire or are overwritten in accordance with normal retention procedures.

Support correspondence

Support messages are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to answer your request, maintain an appropriate support record or comply with a legal obligation.

9. Pro subscriptions and account deletion

Your Habidays account and your Apple App Store or Google Play subscription are separate.

Deleting your Habidays account does not automatically cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription.

If you have an active Pro subscription and want to stop future charges, you should cancel the subscription through the store where you purchased it.

Deleting your Habidays account removes access to account-dependent Pro features, including cloud storage and synchronisation.

Where appropriate, Habidays may offer you the choice to keep your account until the end of an already-paid subscription period before returning to local-only use.

Refunds and remaining subscription access are handled according to the rules of the store through which you purchased Habidays Pro and any applicable consumer law.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

Communication between Habidays and its online services is transmitted using secure encrypted connections where supported by the relevant service.

Your locally stored Habidays journal relies in part on the security protections provided by your device and operating system. You should protect your device with an appropriate password, passcode or biometric lock.

Synced journal information is stored in our cloud database so that the service can process it for backup and synchronisation. Habidays does not claim that journal content is end-to-end encrypted or that it is technically impossible for authorised infrastructure administrators to access stored information.

Access to production data and administrative systems is restricted and should only be used where necessary to operate, secure or support the service.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
